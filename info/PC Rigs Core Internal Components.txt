Cooling & Chassis
CPU Cooler: An air heatsink or all-in-one (AIO) liquid cooler mounted to the processor to prevent thermal throttling.
PC Case (Tower): The outer enclosure that houses, organizes, and protects all internal hardware.
Case Fans & Thermal Paste: Extra airflow fans and conductive compound to transfer heat safely from the CPU to the cooler.

Peripherals & Software
Display & Input: A monitor, keyboard, and mouse.
Operating System: An OS installer and license key (such as Windows 11).
Optional Expansion Cards: Dedicated Wi-Fi/Network cards or sound cards if not built directly into the motherboard.
