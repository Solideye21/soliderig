Duty is an allowance, not a quote

The 15% figure in the calculator is a placeholder for planning. Real charges depend on tariff classification, customs valuation, applicable levies and how the goods are shipped. Get it confirmed by a clearing agent before you commit to a retail price.

Ask for both freight options

Option A — all parts shipped separately, with model numbers and declared values. Option B — a complete assembled PC, quoted with gross weight, carton dimensions, insurance and delivery. The gap between them is usually the deciding factor.

Match before you order

Socket, chipset, memory generation, GPU clearance and PSU headroom. A supplier quote that doesn't list exact model numbers isn't a quote — it's a guess, and it will arrive as a different part.

Questions

Before you order

How long does a build take?

Typically 5–10 working days once all parts are in hand. Fast-moving parts like current-gen GPUs can add a few days if a supplier is out of stock — we'll tell you at quote stage rather than after you've paid.

Why are prices in dollars?

PC components are imported and priced against the dollar, so parts costs move with the exchange rate. We quote in USD for stability and convert to Naira at our current rate, shown on the builds above. A quote locks for 7 days at the rate you were given.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes — nationwide, insured and tracked. Delivery cost depends on location and is added to your quote, never hidden. Larger builds ship in a padded crate with the GPU braced or packed separately.

Can I supply some of my own parts?

Yes. Bring your own GPU, storage or case and we'll build around it. We'll still compatibility-check everything first, and we can't warranty a part we didn't supply — but we do warranty the assembly and labour.

What does the warranty actually cover?

24 months on assembly and labour, plus the full manufacturer warranty on each component (usually 3–10 years on PSUs and coolers, 2–3 on GPUs). If a part fails, we handle the RMA — you don't chase the supplier.

Does it come with Windows installed?

Yes. Clean Windows 11 install, all drivers, BIOS updated, XMP/EXPO enabled, and a benchmark run recorded so you know what "normal" looks like for your machine.

Can I upgrade it later?

That's the point of the tier ladder. Every build list includes what to upgrade next and roughly when — usually storage first, then GPU, then CPU if the platform supports it.

What if I don't know which build to pick?

Send a quote request and describe what you play. If a cheaper build gets you the same experience, we'll say so — that's cheaper for both of us than a build you regret.

Why is the price higher than US retail?

Because the US price is not the price of the PC here. Add international freight, 15% duty and VAT allowance, clearing, assembly labour, a warranty reserve and payment fees, and a $1,500 parts list becomes a $2,672 machine. The calculator shows every line — you can check our arithmetic instead of taking our word for it.

How exactly is the price calculated?

Landed parts = (parts + freight) × (1 + duty). Cost = landed + labour + consumables + packaging + warranty reserve. List price = cost ÷ (1 − margin − payment fee). Nothing is added afterwards, and there is no "we'll see" margin hidden in the parts list.

What if the exchange rate moves before I pay?

A quote locks for 7 days at the rate you were given. After that, or if the Naira-dollar rate moves more than 5%, the balance is recalculated at the rate on the day of order. We tell you this up front rather than springing it at delivery.
